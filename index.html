How to read this series. Each deck takes five landmark publications and, for every one, answers three questions: what problem did it solve, what is the core technical contribution, and why does it matter to someone building LLM and agent systems today — with a diagram of the key idea wherever a picture helps. The six decks are roughly chronological in what they build on, but each stands alone.
